## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🏦 [Quantitative Portfolio Optimization & Market Analysis](https://github.com/losadamiano/markowitz-portfolio-optimization)
**R & Rmetrics**
A comparative study on the **FTSE MIB** (Italian Stock Exchange) across distinct macroeconomic phases (Pre-COVID, COVID-19, and Post-War Inflation).
* **Models:** Modern Portfolio Theory (MPT), Efficient Frontier, Global Minimum Variance, and Tangency Portfolios.
* **Analysis:** Conducted stress tests comparing Long-Only vs. Unlimited Short-Selling constraints and analyzed dynamic covariance matrices for high-volatility regimes.

### 📊 [Operational Efficiency in Italian Manufacturing](https://github.com/losadamiano/JIT-Financial-Benchmarking)
**R & AIDA Database**
[cite_start]Research project investigating the impact of **Just-In-Time (JIT)** production models on the financial performance of Italian manufacturing firms[cite: 5, 12].
* [cite_start]**Methodology:** Extracted and cleaned financial statements for thousands of companies from the **AIDA database**[cite: 13, 50].
* **Advanced Analytics:** Used **Principal Component Analysis (PCA)** for dimensionality reduction and statistical regression to correlate inventory turnover with profitability and liquidity metrics.
* **Insight:** Identified efficiency clusters and analyzed the resilience of lean manufacturing during supply chain disruptions.

### 📉 [Advanced Multi-Model Asset Allocation](https://github.com/losadamiano/multi-model-portfolio-optimization)
**MATLAB**
A comprehensive portfolio optimization framework comparing professional allocation strategies for Nasdaq and NYSE top constituents.
* **Models:** Implementation of **Mean-Variance**, **Black-Litterman**, and Bayesian approaches.
* **Robust Estimation:** Developed a "COMBO" strategy to maximize the Sharpe Ratio and mitigate estimation risk.
* **Audit:** Conducted statistical audits of return distributions to identify leptokurtosis and fat-tail risks.
